My own cliff notes / understanding of the [_what is politics_](TODO) youtube series

_Politics_ relates to decision-making in groups  
word usage sanity check examples:

1. office _politics_ pertains to which people has decision-making power
2. A monarchy / dictatorship is a _political_ system where the kind / tsar / emperor has
   a monopoly on the decisions being made.
   1. All the _political_ power belongs to the king
3. A democracy is a _political_ system where each member / citizen has an equal
   amount of decision-making power (1 man 1 vote, that sorta deal)
   1. _Political_ power is shared amongst the people

Since _left-wing_ and _right-wing_ are political terms, any coherent definition
of the terms as such should have something to do with decision-making in groups as well:

1. _left-wing_ politics refers to political ideas / ideals that tend towards an equality of
   decision-making power
   - a democracy is a _left-wing_ political system
2. _right-wing_ politics refers to political ideas / ideals that tend towards a
   hierarchy / concentration of decision-making power
   - a dictatorship is a _right-wing_ political system
